# Ghola terminal design direction

- Treat the localhost:3011 trading interface as the visual reference: compact, calm, professional, and chart-led.
- Keep one primary market card, pair the chart with market depth, and keep one persistent order ticket.
- Prefer progressive disclosure. Advanced analysis, drawing, automation, and diagnostics should not dominate the default view.
- Market prices must use clean, unslashed numerals and restrained sizing.
- Preserve the certified 3022 execution logic, fail-closed safety gates, authorization checks, evidence, and tests. Visual simplification must never weaken trading controls.
